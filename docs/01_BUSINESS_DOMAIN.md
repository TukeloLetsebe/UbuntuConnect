# UbuntuConnect Business Domain Model

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the business domain of UbuntuConnect.

It explains the real-world concepts, actors, relationships and business rules that govern the platform.

This document serves as the single source of truth for all future architectural, frontend and backend decisions.

Every feature, API endpoint, database entity and user interface must align with this business model.

If uncertainty exists, this document takes precedence over assumptions.

---

# 2. Business Problem

Communities often contain people willing to help.

Communities also contain organizations needing help.

Unfortunately these groups rarely find one another efficiently.

Non-Profit Organisations struggle to

• gain exposure

• recruit volunteers

• receive donations

• communicate their needs

• establish partnerships

Likewise,

Individuals and Businesses struggle to

• discover trustworthy organisations

• find volunteering opportunities

• donate effectively

• participate in community development

UbuntuConnect exists to remove this disconnect.

---

# 3. Core Philosophy

UbuntuConnect follows the African philosophy of Ubuntu.

"I am because we are."

Technology exists to strengthen communities rather than replace them.

The platform measures success through meaningful community engagement rather than user activity alone.

---

# 4. Primary Actors

The platform consists of three primary actor types.

---

## Individual

Represents a person using the platform.

Primary Goals

• Discover organisations

• Follow organisations

• Volunteer

• Donate

• Participate in discussions

• Share campaigns

• Build community

---

## Non-Profit Organisation (NPO)

Represents an organisation operating for community benefit.

Primary Goals

• Build credibility

• Recruit volunteers

• Raise funds

• Publish campaigns

• Share updates

• Manage projects

• Build partnerships

---

## Business

Represents a commercial organisation interested in social impact.

Primary Goals

• Sponsor campaigns

• Donate resources

• Build partnerships

• Participate in CSR initiatives

• Support local communities

---

# 7. Business Rules

Every user must authenticate.

Every profile belongs to exactly one user.

Only authenticated users may perform protected actions.

Only organisations may create campaigns.

Campaigns belong to one organisation.

Posts belong to one creator.

Users may interact only with visible content.

Every action must be auditable.

Business rules take precedence over technical implementation.
